### Day 3-5: Winery Validation
- [ ] **Create list of 30 Walla Walla wineries** to approach
  - Prioritize wineries you have connections with
  - Mix of sizes (large, mid-size, boutique)
  - Include innovative/tech-forward wineries

- [ ] **Schedule 10 coffee meetings** with winery owners/managers
  - Email template:
    ```
    Subject: Quick Coffee? Building Something for Walla Walla Wineries

    Hi [Name],

    I'm [Your Name], a local Walla Walla resident working on a new booking
    platform specifically for wine tourism. Think "Resy for wine country."

    I'd love to buy you coffee and get 15 minutes of your feedback on the
    challenges you face with tasting room reservations. Your insights would
    be invaluable.

    Are you free this week or next?

    Thanks,
    [Your Name]
    ```

- [ ] **Conduct interviews** (bring notebook, not laptop - less formal)
  - Questions to ask:
    1. How do you currently handle reservations? (phone, email, other platform?)
    2. What percentage of your guests book vs. walk in?
    3. What's your no-show rate on walk-ins?
    4. Do you ever turn people away due to capacity?
    5. Have you used any booking platforms? What did/didn't you like?
    6. Would you be interested in a platform that charges just 2% vs. 10-15%?
    7. What features would you need to see before adopting?

- [ ] **Document findings** in spreadsheet:
  - Winery name
  - Current booking method
  - Pain points
  - Interest level (1-10)
  - Would they be a "founding partner"? (Yes/No/Maybe)

**Goal**: Get 5 wineries to verbally commit as founding partners

### Day 6-7: Tourist Validation
- [ ] **Survey 20-30 wine tourists** (if possible)
  - Visit 3-4 tasting rooms (ask permission from owners first)
  - Approach tourists before/after tastings
  - Offer to buy them a glass of wine for 5 min of their time

    2. How did you find/choose the wineries you're visiting today?
    3. Did you book in advance or walk in?

    6. Would you download an app or use a website for wine trip planning?

- [ ] **Online research**
  - Check Google search volume for "Walla Walla wine tasting reservations"
  - Browse TripAdvisor, Yelp reviews for Walla Walla wineries
  - Join wine tourism Facebook groups, observe conversations
  - Note: What are people asking? What problems do they mention?

**Deliverable**: Validation summary (2-page doc with key findings)

## Week 2 (Days 8-14): Legal, Financial, Branding

### Day 8-9: Business Formation
- [ ] **Choose business structure**: LLC (recommended for simplicity)
- [ ] **Register LLC** in Washington State
  - Use Northwest Registered Agent ($200) or LegalZoom ($300)
  - Or DIY via WA Secretary of State website ($200)

- [ ] **Get EIN** (Employer Identification Number)
  - Apply online at IRS.gov (free, takes 10 minutes)

- [ ] **Open business bank account**
  - Bring: EIN letter, LLC formation docs, ID
  - Recommended banks: Chase Business, Bank of America Business
  - Deposit initial capital

- [ ] **Set up accounting**
  - QuickBooks Online ($30/month) or Wave (free)
  - Create chart of accounts
  - Track all expenses from day 1 (investors will want to see this)

**Deliverable**: LLC formed, bank account open, accounting set up

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Waiting for perfection** - Launch with MVP, iterate based on feedback
❌ **Building in isolation** - Show wineries and users the product early and often
❌ **Hiring the cheapest** - Pay for quality developer/designer; bad code is expensive to fix
❌ **Ignoring feedback** - If 3 wineries say they need a feature, build it
❌ **Not tracking metrics** - Investors will ask for data; start tracking from day 1
❌ **Underestimating legal** - Get proper terms of service, privacy policy (use template or lawyer)
❌ **Over-complicating** - Start with Walla Walla only; don't try to launch everywhere

## Mental Model: You're Running Experiments

Think of your first 6 months as **a series of experiments**:

- **Experiment 1**: Will wineries commit to 2% commission? ✅ (If yes, continue)
- **Experiment 2**: Will tourists book online vs. walk-in? ⏳ (Test with MVP)
- **Experiment 3**: Can we build this for $40K? ⏳ (Track spend)
- **Experiment 4**: Will users repeat book? ⏳ (Measure post-launch)
- **Experiment 5**: Can we scale beyond Walla Walla? ⏳ (Month 9+)

Each experiment either **validates** your assumptions or **teaches** you to pivot. Stay flexible.
