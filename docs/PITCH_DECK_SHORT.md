# Slide 1: The Problem

## Wine Tourism is a $60B Industry Stuck in the 1990s

### For Wine Tourists (30M annually)
- Must call wineries individually to book tastings
- No way to see real-time availability
- Uncertain if they'll have space (20-30% turned away)

### For Wineries (3,000+ in US)
- Manual booking management (phone calls, spreadsheets)
- 20-30% no-show rate = lost revenue
- Expensive platforms (Tock: 10-15% commission)
- Wine club management costs $500+/month

**Result:** Tourists frustrated. Wineries losing revenue. No modern solution.

::: notes
$60B market, but wineries still use pen and paper. We're solving both sides of the problem.
:::

## Two Products, One Integrated Platform

### 1. Booking Platform (4% commission)
- Browse wineries by region
- Real-time availability + instant booking
- Prepaid tastings (eliminates no-shows)
- **60% cheaper than competitors**

### 2. Wine Club Management SaaS ($199-799/mo)
- Recurring billing (Stripe Subscriptions)
- Shipment scheduling and tracking
- Member portal (self-service)
- **3× cheaper than WineDirect**

**The Secret:** Booking customers → Wine club members (10-15% conversion)
