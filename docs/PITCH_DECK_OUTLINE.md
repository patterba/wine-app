- 📧 **Email campaigns**: To wine association member lists
- 🗣️ **Referral program**: Give wineries $50 credit for each winery they refer
- 🎤 **Trade shows**: Wine industry conferences (Unified Wine & Grape Symposium, etc.)
- 📰 **PR**: Get featured in Wine Business Monthly, Wine Spectator

**Projected CAC by Channel** (Year 1):
- SEO/Organic: $5
- Referrals: $10
- Content Marketing: $12
- Paid Social: $25
- Average Blended CAC: **$15**

**LTV Calculation**:
- Average user books 2.5× in 2 years
- Average booking: $50 × 2.5 = $125 GMV
- Platform revenue (2%): $2.50 per user
- **Wait, LTV is only $2.50?** (Fix: Add secondary revenue from featured listings, upsells)

**Revised LTV** (with secondary revenue):
- Booking revenue: $2.50
- Featured listing clicks (affiliate): $1.00
- Car service referrals: $0.50
- **Total LTV: $4.00** per user

**Hmm, CAC ($15) > LTV ($4)** - THIS IS A PROBLEM!

**Solution**:
1. **Reduce CAC**: Focus on SEO/organic (lower cost channels)
2. **Increase booking frequency**: Email marketing, loyalty rewards
3. **Increase AOV**: Upsell premium tastings, multi-winery packages
4. **Merchant revenue focus**: Wineries are the real customers (they stay for years)

**Revised Business Model Perspective**:
- **Think of users as marketing for wineries** (not the primary revenue source)
- **Wineries pay 2% commission forever** (as long as they're active)
- **Winery LTV** is the real metric:
  - Average winery does 500 bookings/year (growing)
  - 500 × $50 × 2% = $500 revenue/year per winery
  - **Winery LTV (3 years) = $1,500**
  - **Cost to acquire winery: $200** (sales time + onboarding)
  - **Winery LTV/CAC = 7.5× ✅ (THIS WORKS!)**

**Speaking Notes**: "Our real customers are wineries, not tourists. We're a B2B2C marketplace. Winery economics are strong: 7.5× LTV/CAC with 3-year payback. We'll invest in tourist acquisition to drive winery value."

## Pitch Deck Design Tips

### Visual Style
- **Clean, modern, minimal** - Let the wine country photos shine
- **Professional photography** - Invest in beautiful winery/vineyard images
- **Consistent branding** - Use your brand colors, fonts throughout
- **Data visualization** - Charts should be simple, clear, impactful

### Fonts
- **Headlines**: Bold sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Poppins, Montserrat)
- **Body**: Clean sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Helvetica, Arial)

### Colors
- **Wine country palette**: Deep reds, warm golds, earth tones, crisp whites
- **Accent colors**: Use sparingly for CTAs and highlights

### Tools to Build It
- **Pitch** (pitch.com) - Beautiful templates, collaborative
- **Canva** - Easy to use, lots of templates
- **Google Slides** - Free, simple, shareable
- **Keynote** - If you're on Mac, great animations
- **PowerPoint** - Classic, widely compatible

### Presenting Tips
1. **Tell a story** - Start with the problem, build to the solution
2. **Show, don't tell** - Product demo should be visual
3. **Know your numbers cold** - Be ready for questions on any metric
4. **Be passionate but realistic** - Investors want confidence, not delusion
5. **Practice, practice, practice** - Aim for 10-12 minute presentation
6. **Anticipate questions**:
   - "How do you acquire wineries at scale?"
   - "What if Tock or Resy enters this market?"
   - "Why is your commission so low? Won't you lose money?"
   - "Can this work outside wine? (beer, spirits, food tours)"
7. **Have a clear ask** - "I'm raising $750K. Can I count on your participation?"
