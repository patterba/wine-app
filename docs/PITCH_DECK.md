# The Problem

## For Wine Tourists

- 🔍 Hard to discover wineries and check availability
- 📞 Must call wineries individually (during business hours only)
- ❌ Uncertain if they'll have space (walk-up risk)
- 🗺️ Fragmented trip planning (no central platform)

## For Wineries

- 📝 Manual booking management (phone calls, spreadsheets)
- 👻 20-30% no-show rate on walk-ins = lost revenue
- 📊 No capacity planning tools
- 💸 Pay 10-15% commission to platforms like Tock

::: notes
Wine tourism is a $60B global industry, but it's stuck in the 1990s. We're solving both sides.
:::

---

# The Solution

## For Wine Tourists

- ✅ Discover wineries by region with rich profiles
- ✅ See real-time availability and book instantly
- ✅ Prepay tasting fee for guaranteed reservation
- ✅ Plan multi-winery itineraries

## For Wineries

- ✅ Automated booking management with capacity controls
- ✅ Prepaid tastings reduce no-shows to near-zero
- ✅ Marketing exposure to qualified wine tourists
- ✅ **Just 4% commission** (vs. 10-15% competitors)

We're a two-sided marketplace. Tourists get certainty. Wineries get prepaid revenue at the lowest fee in the market.

# Unit Economics

## Standard Booking (70% of volume)
- Revenue: $2.00 (4% commission)
- Costs: $2.10 (Stripe, hosting, support)
- **Margin: -$0.10** (nearly break-even)

## Featured Booking (30% of volume)
- Revenue: $7.00 ($5 premium + $2 commission)
- Costs: $2.10
- **Margin: $4.90**

## Blended Average
- **$1.40 profit per booking**
- Improves to $2.40 with 50% premium adoption
