**Pricing Tiers:**
- **Featured Badge**: $149/month
  - Highlighted in search with badge
  - Top 3 placement in results
  - 2-3× more profile views

- **Premium Listing**: $299/month
  - Everything in Featured
  - Homepage carousel rotation
  - Email newsletter inclusion
  - "Top Pick" badge

- **Exclusive Spotlight**: $599/month
  - Everything in Premium
  - Dedicated top placement on region page
  - Social media promotion
  - Blog spotlight article
  - Email blast to users

**Why Wineries Will Pay:**
- Simple ROI math: Featured gets you 10 extra bookings/month at $50 each = $500 revenue
- Cost: $149/month
- Net benefit: $351/month → 3.4× ROI ✅

**Revenue Projections:**
- Year 1: $18K (10 wineries)
- Year 2: $96K (40 wineries)
- Year 3: $270K (100 wineries)

**Implementation:**
- **Complexity**: Low (just UI badges + sorting algorithm)
- **Development Time**: 2 weeks
- **Cost**: $3-5K
- **Launch**: Month 7 (immediately after MVP)
- **Margin**: 90%+ (almost pure profit)

### 2. Wine Club Management Platform 🔥🔥 (GAME CHANGER)

**What It Is:**
A full SaaS platform for wineries to manage wine club memberships, recurring billing, shipments, and member communications.

**Why This Is Huge:**
- Wine clubs generate **30-50% of winery DTC revenue**
- Current solutions are expensive ($300-1,000/month) and outdated
- Massive market: 3,000 US wineries with clubs, $1.5-6B in annual sales
- High switching costs = **sticky, recurring revenue**

**Core Features:**
1. **Membership Management**
   - Create tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum)
   - Member database
   - Lifecycle management (onboarding, pause, cancellation)

2. **Recurring Billing**
   - Automatic credit card charging (monthly/quarterly/annually)
   - Failed payment retry logic
   - Invoicing and receipts

3. **Shipment Management**
   - Schedule seasonal releases
   - Track fulfillment status
   - Integration with ShipCompliant (wine shipping compliance)
   - Tracking notifications

4. **Member Portal**
   - Members can update payment/shipping info
   - Pause or skip shipments
   - Refer friends
   - Shop additional wines

5. **Communication Tools**
   - Email campaigns to members
   - Shipment notifications
   - SMS alerts (optional)

6. **Analytics Dashboard**
   - MRR (monthly recurring revenue)
   - Churn rate
   - Member LTV
   - Retention cohorts

**Pricing:**
- **Starter**: $199/month (up to 100 members)
- **Growth**: $399/month (up to 500 members)
- **Enterprise**: $799/month (unlimited members)
- **Plus**: 1.5% transaction fee on wine club charges

**Revenue Per Winery:**
- Subscription: $400/month average = $4,800/year
- Transaction fees: $600/year average (1.5% of $40K in club sales)
- **Total: $5,400/year per winery**

**Revenue Projections:**
- Year 1: $6K (launch late, only 4 months of revenue)
- Year 2: $248K (40 wineries)
- Year 3: **$1.02M** (150 wineries) 💰💰💰

**Why Wineries Will Switch:**
- **50% cheaper** than WineDirect ($400 vs. $800/month)
- **Modern UX** (built in 2025 vs. 2010)
- **Integrated** with booking platform (seamless tasting → wine club funnel)
- **Better support** (small team, high-touch service)

**Implementation:**
- **Complexity**: Medium-High (recurring billing, shipment tracking, member portal)
- **Development Time**: 3-4 months
- **Cost**: $30-40K
- **Launch**: Month 9-12 (beta with 3 wineries)
- **Margin**: 85%+ on subscription, plus transaction fees
- **Break-Even**: 125 wineries (achievable Year 2-3)

**Competitive Landscape:**
- **WineDirect**: $500-1,000/month (expensive, clunky)
- **Commerce7**: $300-600/month (modern but complex)
- **VinSuite**: $400+/month (enterprise-focused)
- **Spreadsheets**: Free (manual, error-prone)
- **Our Advantage**: Best pricing + best UX + integrated with bookings
